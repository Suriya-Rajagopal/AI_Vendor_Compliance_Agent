=====================================================
Vendor Compliance Policy Handbook
=====================================================

Section 1 - Website Security

Rule 1:
Every vendor website must use HTTPS.

Rule 2:
HTTP websites are considered non-compliant.

-----------------------------------------------------

Section 2 - Privacy

Rule 3:
Every vendor should publish a Privacy Policy.

Rule 4:
Privacy Policy should be publicly accessible.

-----------------------------------------------------

Section 3 - Contact Information

Rule 5:
Every vendor should provide Contact Information.

Rule 6:
Contact page should contain email or phone number.

-----------------------------------------------------

Section 4 - Legal

Rule 7:
Vendor should publish Terms and Conditions.

Rule 8:
Vendor should publish Cookie Policy if applicable.

-----------------------------------------------------

Section 5 - Information Security

Rule 9:
ISO 27001 Certification is recommended.

Rule 10:
SOC 2 Certification is an added advantage.

-----------------------------------------------------

Section 6 - Regional Compliance

Rule 11:
European vendors should comply with GDPR.

Rule 12:
US vendors should comply with applicable privacy laws.

-----------------------------------------------------

Section 7 - Final Decision

If HTTPS, Privacy Policy and Contact Information are available,
vendor can be marked as LOW RISK.

If one or more mandatory rules are missing,
vendor should be marked as MEDIUM or HIGH RISK.
